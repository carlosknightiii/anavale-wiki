The Magic of Anavale

The Gigglegloom

Magic in Anavale isn't a tool. It's alive. The Gigglegloom runs through the soil and the sky and the river water and the fur of small animals who hum. It responds to emotion. It has opinions. It occasionally does things nobody asked it to do, and the results are almost always absurd and almost never deadly.

It comes in four kinds. Each one has a color. Each one has a personality.

In its raw, unattached form — before it becomes Bubbleseed or Featherflow or anything else — the Gigglegloom is called the Prior. Pure white. Blinding. The holiest thing in Anavale, and the one thing in this world that cannot be grey.

Color in Anavale is not decoration. It is health, vitality, and magic all at once — a living measure of how much of the Gigglegloom a person, a place, or a creature still carries. A world full of color is a world that is alive and well. You will understand why this matters before the end of this page.

Bubbleseed
Earth · Growth · Joy
Warm and generous and slightly too much. Smells like soil after rain and, sometimes, like something baking. Emotionally attached to whoever uses it. Will overshoot.
Featherflow
Wind · Water · Freedom
Never goes in a straight line. Resists being caged, contained, or asked to explain itself. Some Wanderkeep rangers believe it may slow early Fading — the Gigglegloom has not confirmed this.
Steelfist
Metal · Resolve · Order
Rewards discipline. Punishes sloppiness. Its grey shines — cold, geometric, precise. There is another kind of grey in this world. You will know the difference when you see it.
Flamerage
Fire · Destruction · Fury
Responds to emotion before intent. Spectacular. The only type that can fight the grey directly — the only one that can temporarily overpower Stage 1 Fading. Use carefully. Or don't.

Every colorful thing is an act of defiance.
Every grey thing is a loss.

The Living World

The Creatures of Anavale

Anavale is loud. Its creatures are everywhere, and they have feelings about things. Harming one unprovoked is considered unconscionable by virtually everyone on the continent — and enforced accordingly. This is not a law so much as a deeply held continental opinion backed by centuries of consequences.

Bumble Frogs
Hum constantly. Harmonize with each other accidentally. Will join any song within earshot without being invited. Cannot match pitch but somehow always sound right.
Fluffets
Tiny squirrels. Say "bwip." Leave acorn gifts for people they like. Consider this a formal gesture of alliance.
Tinywings
Thumb-sized dragons. Cannot fly straight. Extraordinarily proud. Will attempt to fly straight again immediately after crashing.
Bounce Beetles
Ricochet off surfaces with a "boing" sound. Always arrive at the correct destination. Nobody knows how.
Sparklings
Hummingbirds that leave color trails in the air. Each one is permanently aligned to a single type of the Gigglegloom — gold-rose for Bubbleseed, aquamarine for Featherflow, cold indigo for Steelfist, ember-edged red for Flamerage. Where they are, the Gigglegloom is healthy. Where they aren't, the Wanderkeep starts asking questions.
Pocketmoles
The most common pet in Caparia. Finds sad people and sits on their foot. Stays until things are better.
Hollowmoths
Large. Ash-grey. Native to Dimmed territory. Children are inexplicably unafraid of them. If you see one somewhere new, the Dimming has already arrived.

The World of Pogglewog

Four Regions

Pogglewog is a continent of four great regions — each shaped by its climate, its magic, and the nations that call it home. Every region tells a different story. Every story is still being written.

Region
Caparia
The colorful heartlands
Lush meadows, sparkling rivers, and the most colorful cities in Anavale. The Confederation was built here. Color maintenance is not a tradition — it is law.
Region
Nombi
The frozen, aurora-lit north
Dense forests, icy mountains, and skies painted by the aurora. The north is stoic and beautiful and dangerous in equal measure. Honor is currency here.
Region
Sohot
The ancient, blazing south
Desert dunes, ancient ceremony, and the weight of a kingdom that has never forgotten a single thing. The south's colors have always been vivid. They have been looking slightly less so, lately.
Region
Jugabi
The living jungle southwest
The Dodooti Rainforest produces more ambient Gigglegloom than any other terrain type on the continent. The jungle has opinions. The jungle is always right.

The People of Anavale

Voices from across the world

Anavale is not short of opinions. Chancellors, archivists, field assessors, bureaucrats, and at least one very famous frog have thoughts about the current state of things. This is what they're saying.

Everyone in Anavale has an opinion. The Gigglegloom makes sure of it.

Chancellor Mira Voss
Rotating Chancellor · The Caldenric Accord · Solenveil
"The Confederation has received seventeen incident reports from the Wanderkeep this year. We have acknowledged all seventeen. I would like to note, for the record, that acknowledging a report and doing nothing about it are not the same thing. I would also like to note that I am currently being prevented from doing something about it by Forms 12-G through 12-K."
Archivist Pellum
Grand Archivist · The Stillkeep · Lightcrak
"The Stillkeep has been searching for Veilhaven for forty years. We have not found it. I want to be clear that this is not because we have been looking in the wrong place. We have been looking in the right place. The right place simply does not wish to be found yet, and we have decided to respect that. We are patient people."
Headmistress Sova Drin
Director-General · The Gigglegloom Conclave · Prismhold
"The Gigglegloom Conclave detected an anomaly in the southern color-health readings fourteen weeks ago. We are currently completing the required internal review before any external communication is appropriate. The process has seventeen steps. We are on step four. I am aware of how that sounds."
Strix
Northern Trade Controller · Frostgate · Nombi
"Northern trade has never been more stable. I want the southern merchants to understand that. The routes are open, the tolls are fair, and Frostgate has never been quieter. Everyone should find that reassuring." She paused. "The winter color surveys were exceptional this year. I've had them archived."
Petra Kindwell
Senior Field Assessor · The Kindpact · Caparia
"We maintain a registry of every known Husk location in Anavale. It is not a short list. We update it every month. People ask us why we keep track when there is nothing to be done for a Husk. We keep track because someone should. Because if nobody counts the losses, it becomes very easy to pretend they are not happening."
The Formery
Bureau of Thorough Consideration · All Regions · Est. Before Anyone Can Confirm
"This office has, on seventeen separate occasions, filed Form 1-A (Entity Classification) in connection with entities referred to internally as The Unseen. On each occasion, the completed form was returned to this office stamped: ENTITY DECLINES TO RESPOND. This office has processed each return in accordance with standard procedure and filed Form 1-B (Acknowledgment of Non-Response) accordingly. A further Form 1-A is currently in preparation. The process continues."

The Ones Who Were Here First

The Five Gods of Anavale

The gods of Anavale are not distant. They are not metaphors. They are present, opinionated, and aware that something is very wrong. Four of them are doing something about it. The fifth is what the trouble is.

Before the world had color, there were three. Then came the Partition — and everything changed.

The Five

Meet the Gods

Oro
Color · Joy · Laughter
The most widely worshipped god in Anavale. Active and embarrassingly invested in the wellbeing of creatures. Cannot enter Dimmed territory — which means the Vareth has already taken places from him. This is not defeat. But it is a real boundary.
The Brightcreed
Nara
Wild Magic · Living Things
God of the wild Gigglegloom — the kind that grows from old forests without anyone's help. Diminished by the Partition. Thyun and Solvara were sheared from her in the collision and became their own gods. She survived. She is still a river.
The Brightcreed
Thyun
Memory · Deep Time · Patience
Born from the Partition — sheared from Nara as an independent god. Worshipped by the Stillkeep, who have been trying to transcribe one of Thyun's thoughts for eight hundred years. They are close. Probably.
The Stillkeep
Solvara
Secrets · Shadow · Hidden Truth
Born from the Partition — Nara's shadow-self, sheared into full separate divinity. Worshipped by the Veilborn, whose practices are not public knowledge. Solvara knows what actually happened at the Partition and has told no one. The Veilborn consider this correct.
The Veilborn
Grak
Once: Order. Now: Silence.
The fallen god. Emptied of everything by the Partition except hunger for control. The Vareth is what remained. Grak has no throne, no face, no voice — only the grey that spreads from his absence outward. He does not want conquest. He wants Quietude.
No worshippers

The Faiths of Anavale

Three Ways of Understanding

The gods are real. The disagreement is about what that means. Three faiths have built their entire understanding of the world around the same five gods — and reached very different conclusions.

☀
The Brightcreed
Oro · Nara · The Light of the World
"Joy is not frivolous. Color is not decoration. Every saturated thing in this world is an act of defiance against the grey."
The most widespread faith in Anavale. Brightcreed temples are open fields — no roof, no walls, just sky. They believe Grak's jealousy was always its weakness, and that the Partition was inevitable.
◈
The Stillkeep
Thyun · Memory · The Long View
"The Partition was preventable. Grak's grief was legitimate. The world that exists now is the result of a failure to listen — and we must remember that."
Monastic record-keepers and patient observers. Stillkeep temples are stone libraries carved into mountains. They have been trying to transcribe one of Thyun's complete thoughts for eight hundred years. They believe they are close.
◑
The Veilborn
Solvara · Shadow · Hidden Truth
"Solvara knows what actually happened at the Partition. She has told no one. We consider this correct."
Secretive, decentralized, rumored to be everywhere. The Veilborn do not confirm their practices. Their seat of power — Veilhaven — does not officially exist. The Stillkeep has been searching for it for forty years.

Your story begins soon

Anavale is waiting. Its meadows are loud with Bumble Frogs and Bounce Beetles and the particular chaos of a world that is very, very alive. Its cities are painted in colors that mean something. Its people are funny and complicated and trying their best.

And at the edges of the map, where the grey is arriving, something needs to be done about that.
