The Magic of Anavale

The Gigglegloom

Magic in Anavale isn't a tool. It's alive. The Gigglegloom runs through the soil and the sky and the river water and the fur of small animals who hum. It responds to emotion. It has opinions. It occasionally does things nobody asked it to do, and the results are almost always absurd and almost never deadly.

It comes in four kinds. Each one has a color. Each one has a personality. When the colors start to fade — when grey creeps in at the edges — something is very, very wrong.

Bubbleseed
Earth · Growth · Joy
Warm and generous and slightly too much. Smells like soil after rain. Emotionally attached to whoever uses it. Will overshoot.
Featherflow
Wind · Water · Freedom
Never goes in a straight line. Resists being caged. Has been known to counteract early Fading, though it won't admit that's what it's doing.
Steelfist
Metal · Resolve · Order
Does exactly what it's told. Rewards discipline. Punishes sloppiness. Its grey shines — don't confuse it with the other kind of grey.
Flamerage
Fire · Destruction · Fury
Responds to emotion before intent. Spectacular. The only type that can temporarily overpower Stage 1 Fading. Use carefully. Or don't.

The Living World

The Creatures of Anavale

Anavale is loud. Its creatures are everywhere, and they have feelings about things. Harming one unprovoked will unite the Greenvast Tribes against you within 72 hours. This is considered a reasonable policy by most of the continent.

Bumble Frogs
Hum constantly. Harmonize with each other accidentally. Will harmonize with rivers, wind, and one very famous bridge.
Fluffets
Tiny squirrels. Say "bwip." Leave acorn gifts for people they like. Consider this a formal gesture of alliance.
Tinywings
Thumb-sized dragons. Cannot fly straight. Extraordinarily proud. Will attempt to fly straight again immediately after crashing.
Bounce Beetles
Ricochet off surfaces with a "boing" sound. Always arrive at the correct destination. Nobody knows how.
Pocketmoles
The most common pet in Caparia. Finds sad people and sits on their foot. Stays until things are better.
Hollowmoths
Large. Ash-grey. Native to Dimmed territory. If you see one somewhere new — the Dimming has already arrived.

The Continent of Pogglewog

Four Regions, One World

Anavale spans the continent of Pogglewog: lush central meadows, frozen northern wastes, blazing southern deserts, and a jungle so alive it has opinions. Each region has its own colors, its own magic, and its own complicated relationship with the Gigglegloom.

Region
Caparia
The colorful heartlands
Lush meadows, sparkling rivers, and the most colorful cities in Anavale. The Confederation was built here. Color maintenance is not a tradition — it is law.
Region
Nombi
The frozen, aurora-lit north
Dense forests, icy mountains, and skies painted by the aurora. The north is stoic and beautiful and dangerous in equal measure. Honor is currency here.
Region
Sohot
The ancient, blazing south
Desert dunes, ancient ruins, and the weight of a fallen empire. The south remembers everything. The south is also on fire, mostly.
Region
Jugabi
The living jungle southwest
The Dodooti Rainforest produces more ambient Gigglegloom than any other terrain type on the continent. The jungle has opinions. The jungle is always right.

The People of Anavale

Voices from across the world

Anavale is full of people who are funny and complicated and trying their best. Some of them are in charge of things. Some of them are very famous frogs. All of them have something to say about what's happening out there.

Chancellor Mira Voss
Rotating Chancellor · The Caldenric Accord · Solenveil
"The Confederation has received seventeen incident reports from the Wanderkeep this year. We have acknowledged all seventeen. I would like to note, for the record, that acknowledging a report and doing nothing about it are not the same thing. I would also like to note that I am currently being prevented from doing something about it by Forms 12-G through 12-K."
Archivist Pellum
Grand Archivist · The Stillkeep · Lightcrak
"The Stillkeep has been searching for Veilhaven for forty years. We have not found it. I want to be clear that this is not because we have been looking in the wrong place. We have been looking in the right place. The right place simply does not wish to be found yet, and we have decided to respect that. We are patient people."
Headmistress Sova Drin
Director-General · The Gigglegloom Conclave · Prismhold
"The Gigglegloom Conclave detected an anomaly in the southern color-health readings fourteen weeks ago. We are currently completing the required internal review before any external communication is appropriate. The process has seventeen steps. We are on step four. I am aware of how that sounds."
Strix
Northern Trade Controller · Frostgate · Nombi
"Northern trade has never been more stable. I want the southern merchants to understand that. The routes are open, the tolls are fair, and Frostgate has never been quieter. Everyone should find that reassuring."
Petra Kindwell
Senior Field Assessor · The Kindpact · Caparia
"We maintain a registry of every known Husk location in Anavale. It is not a short list. We update it every month. People ask us why we keep track when there is nothing to be done for a Husk. We keep track because someone should. Because if nobody counts the losses, it becomes very easy to pretend they are not happening."

Your story begins soon

Anavale is waiting. Its meadows are loud with Bumble Frogs and Bounce Beetles and the particular chaos of a world that is very, very alive. Its cities are painted in colors that mean something. Its people are funny and complicated and trying their best.

And at the edges of the map, where the colors are starting to go quiet, something needs to be done about that.

Who you are in this world — what you care about, what you're afraid of, what makes you laugh — that's the part you get to decide.
