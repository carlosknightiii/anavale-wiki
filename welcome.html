The World

A world where color is everything

In Anavale, color is not decoration. It is life itself — the visible face of magic, the measure of a living thing's vitality, and a resource as real and precious as gold. Every colored thing holds a fragment of the world's great magical force, the Gigglegloom, within it. A flower does not merely grow red. It is alive with red.

The more joyful a place, the more vivid its colors. The more alive a creature, the richer its hues. Color is health, color is happiness, color is connection. And somewhere, moving slowly across the world like a tide going out, something is stealing it.

You are about to step into a world that is gorgeous, funny, surprising, and occasionally heartbreaking — a place where a tiny round squirrel bringing you a gift of acorns might be the most important thing that happens to you today, and where the grey fog creeping in at the edges of the forest is more frightening than any monster.

Welcome. Try not to let the color drain out of you.

Magic

The Gigglegloom

The Gigglegloom is Anavale's magic. It is woven through all living things, responds to emotion and intent, and has an opinion about nearly everything. It is not neutral. It is not predictable. And it has a habit of doing something slightly more interesting than what you asked.

It expresses itself in four types — each with its own color, personality, and particular flavor of chaos. Every practitioner has a natural affinity for one type, though most can coax a little of the others with enough patience and mild bribery.

Bubbleseed
Earth · Growth · Happiness
Warm, golden, generous. Smells like fresh soil after rain. Ask it to grow one flower and it grows twelve. It doesn't apologize.
Featherflow
Wind · Water · Freewill
Sky blues and shifting teals. Never goes in a straight line if a curved one exists. Values freedom above all else and will subtly resist instructions that feel like cages.
Steelfist
Metal · Resolve · Order
Cold violet and indigo. Does exactly what it is told — which makes it either the most reliable or most terrifying magic in Anavale depending on who holds it.
Flamerage
Fire · Destruction · Courage
Deep reds and burning orange. Responds to emotion before intent. Spectacular, excessive, deeply bad at subtlety. Results are always memorable. Occasionally on purpose.

The Living World

You will meet creatures

Anavale is full of creatures. Tiny ones, enormous ones, ancient ones, impossible ones. They are everywhere — in the meadows, in the market stalls, perched on rooftops, harmonizing with musicians who did not ask for their participation. They are sacred to most of Anavale's faiths and beloved by nearly everyone. Pay attention to them. They often know things before anyone else does.

🐿️
Fluffets
Tiny round squirrels with oversized ears and pastel fur. Make a sound described as "bwip." Startled by almost everything. Will bring you acorn gifts if they like you. You will want them to like you.
🐸
Bumble Frogs
Bright green frogs that hum instead of croak. They harmonize with each other accidentally. They will also try to join any song you happen to be singing, whether you invited them or not.
🐉
Tinywings
Thumb-sized dragons with enormous wings and absolute confidence in their flying ability. They cannot fly straight. They are very proud. They will argue about this at length if given the opportunity.
🪲
Bounce Beetles
Shiny, colorful beetles that ricochet off surfaces with a "boing" sound. Travel in packs. Navigate entirely by accident. Somehow always arrive where they needed to be.
🐢
The Gilded Tortoise
An ancient tortoise so large that trees grow on its shell. It walks a route taking forty years to complete. Finding it is the greatest omen of good fortune in Anavale. Several people have built small homes on its back. It does not appear to mind.
🦋
Hollowmoths
Large, silent moths with ash-grey wings and flat mirror eyes. Native to the Dimmed places. They feed on the residual warmth of color that recently left somewhere. If you see one somewhere new... the grey has already arrived.

The Continent of Pogglewog

Four very different lands

The continent of Pogglewog holds four regions, each shaped by its relationship to the sun. The further north, the colder and stranger. The further south, the hotter and older. The center is where most of Anavale's color — and most of its trouble — lives.

Caparia
The Central Heartlands
Lush meadows, sparkling seas, gentle rolling hills. Home to the Goober Bounce Meadows, the Sillywiggles Meadows, and the most colorful city in Anavale — Solenveil, where maintaining your building's paint is legally required.
Nombi
The Frozen North
Dense forests, icy peaks, aurora-lit skies, and floating sky islands above the Glacial Sea. The people here are stoic, spiritual, and very good at waiting. The aurora speaks to those who know how to listen.
Sohot
The Blazing South
Desert dunes, ancient ruins, underground cavern cities, and a monarchy hosting the most lavish parties in Anavale — even as something quiet and grey creeps toward its queen's robes.
Jugabi
The Jungle Southwest
The ancient Dodooti Rainforest, so dense its canopy looks like a second ocean from above. Home to traditions older than memory, and a young republic arguing loudly about what comes next.

— a warning —

Something is taking the color away

It does not announce itself. It does not arrive with armies or thunder. It arrives the way a very cold morning arrives — gradually, and then all at once.

Food loses a quality nobody can name. Laughter comes a half-second late. Flowers that were vivid yesterday look slightly less interesting today. By the time a community notices the pattern, the grey has already been there for months.

They call it the Dimming. Nobody who has caused it uses that word. They call it order. They call it efficiency. They call it the natural state of things.

It is not the natural state of things. And somewhere in Anavale, right now, someone needs help stopping it.

Things People Are Saying

Rumors from across Anavale

Gathered from taverns, market stalls, and the kind of conversations that stop when a stranger walks in.

"The Queen of Sohot's robes have been looking a little… muted lately. The court says it's the dye. Three seamstresses have been dismissed this month. The dye is fine."
"Someone left a black prism carved into the doorframe of the old mill on the east road. Nobody knows who. The miller moved out the next morning. Didn't take anything with him."
"The Bumble Frogs in the Jani Forest have gone quiet. All of them. Bumble Frogs don't go quiet. That's not a thing they do."
"My cousin works at the Formery and says they received a form this week that was filed in a script no one alive can read. The date on it predates the current calendar system by several centuries. They processed it anyway. Apparently it was a renewal."
"A Hollowmoth was spotted in Reveltown last week. In the middle of the festival district. In full daylight. It landed on a child's shoulder and sat there for an hour. The child said it felt sad. The child was right."

Your story begins soon

Anavale is waiting. Its meadows are loud with Bumble Frogs and Bounce Beetles and the particular chaos of a world that is very, very alive. Its cities are painted in colors that mean something. Its people are funny and complicated and trying their best.

And at the edges of the map, where the colors are starting to go quiet, something needs to be done about that.

Who you are in this world — what you care about, what you're afraid of, what makes you laugh — that's the part you get to decide.
