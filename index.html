Introduction

Welcome, Traveller

A practical guide to the continent of Pogglewog and the wider world of Anavale

You are reading the Anavale Traveller's Compendium — a collection of observations, records, and accumulated wisdom gathered by scholars, wanderers, merchants, and at least one extremely well-travelled tortoise. It is not complete. It is not official. It is, however, honest, which is more than can be said for several of the Formery's pamphlets.

Anavale is a world that rewards attention. Pay attention to the color of things. Pay attention to the creatures. Pay attention when the Bumble Frogs go quiet — they are almost never quiet, and when they are, something has happened or is about to.

"The world is full of color and it wants to stay that way. Assist it where you can." — Common Brightcreed greeting, Caparia

This compendium is organized into sections covering the world's magic, its regions, its creatures, its organizations, and a final section of rumours that the editorial committee insists on including despite ongoing disagreement about their accuracy.

Use the navigation on the left to explore. Begin wherever your curiosity takes you. That is, after all, very much in the spirit of Anavale.

Magic

The Gigglegloom

The living magical force woven through all things

The Gigglegloom is what most people mean when they say "magic" in Anavale, though scholars and the Gigglegloom Conclave's licensing department would like it noted that this is an oversimplification they find professionally upsetting.

The Gigglegloom is not a tool. It is not a resource in the conventional sense. It is a living force woven through every thing that exists in Anavale — through the roots of trees, the water in rivers, the warmth of sunlight, and the bodies of every creature from the largest Glacial Serpent to the smallest Fluffet. It responds to emotion. It responds to intent. It has opinions about what it is asked to do.

"Asking the Gigglegloom nicely does not guarantee results. But it does seem to improve them." — Wanderkeep Field Notes, Vol. 44

Practitioners — those who can consciously direct the Gigglegloom — develop a natural affinity for one of four types, each with its own character, color, and personality. Wild Surges — moments when the Gigglegloom does something without being asked — occur occasionally in areas of high concentration and generally produce effects that are dramatic, harmless, and very difficult to explain afterward.

✦
Bubbleseed
Earth · Growth · Happiness
Warm golden magic. Generous to a fault. Smells like fresh soil after rain. Tends to overshoot — ask for a flower, receive twelve. Common among healers, farmers, and optimistic people generally.
Featherflow
Wind · Water · Freewill
Sky blues and shifting teals. Never goes straight. Values freedom and will subtly resist instructions that feel like cages. Common among navigators, scouts, and people who are very difficult to pin down.
Steelfist
Metal · Resolve · Order
Cold violets and indigos. Does exactly what it is told. The most reliable and the most terrifying depending on the caster. Common among soldiers, historians, and oracles.
Flamerage
Fire · Destruction · Courage
Deep reds and burning orange. Responds to emotion before intent. Spectacular, excessive, deeply bad at subtlety. Common among warriors, performers, and those described as overconfident.
Regarding the Gigglegloom Conclave
All use of the Gigglegloom within governed territories technically requires a license from the Gigglegloom Conclave. The application form is Form 7-A. Form 7-A requires Form 7-B as supporting documentation. Form 7-B requires Form 7-A. This has been noted. It has not been resolved.

Magic

Color & The Dimming

What color means in Anavale, and what its absence means

In Anavale, color is not decoration. It is the Gigglegloom made visible. Every colored thing holds a fragment of the world's magic within it. This is not metaphorical. A red apple is alive with red in a way that a grey stone is not. The vibrancy of a place reflects the health of its Gigglegloom — and the health of its joy.

A community full of color is, in the most literal sense, a community full of life. A community losing its color is a community in distress, whether or not it has noticed yet.

"When the paint starts fading faster than it should, call the Wanderkeep. Don't wait until you can't remember why you painted it in the first place." — Brightcreed practical wisdom, Caparia

The Fading is the first stage of color loss — colors muting slightly, joy becoming a little harder to access, laughter arriving a moment late. Most people in affected regions live at this stage without realizing anything is wrong. It is subtle by design.

The Dimming is the second stage. Full greyscale. Joy is not felt — only remembered. People and creatures at this stage continue to function, continue to eat and sleep and speak, but the light has gone out behind their eyes. Grey Fluffets still try to bring acorn gifts. The acorns are also grey.

There is no third stage. The Dimmed do not become something else. They simply remain — in a world they can no longer feel. This is the goal of those who cause the Dimming. Not conquest. Not cruelty for its own sake. Simply quietude.

Signs of the Dimming
In order of increasing concern: colors appearing slightly muted; food losing an unnamed quality; Bumble Frogs going quiet; Hollowmoths appearing in daylight; animals going very still without apparent cause; a small perfect black prism carved somewhere it wasn't yesterday.
Theology

The Gods

As understood by the faithful, the scholars, and those caught in between

Anavale has five gods, though theologians will argue at length about whether certain of them are truly independent deities or aspects of others. This argument has been ongoing for three hundred years. It has not reached a conclusion. The gods themselves have not weighed in, which the Veilborn consider significant and everyone else considers inconclusive.

Oro
God of Color, Joy & Laughter
The most beloved god in Anavale. Sends signs through bursts of unexpected color. Loves creatures with an enthusiasm that other gods find slightly excessive. Cannot enter Dimmed territory — color drains from anything Oro touches there.
Worshipped by: The Brightcreed (most widely). Temples: open fields.
Nara
Goddess of Wild Magic & Living Things
Curious, mischievous, unpredictable — much like the Gigglegloom itself. Neither fully good nor fully neutral. Was fractured in the divine event called the Partition; two aspects of her became independent gods. She has not fully recovered. She does not discuss it.
Appears as: different things to different observers. Children usually report bright green.
Thyun
God of Memory, Deep Time & Patience
Born from the Partition — Nara's capacity for memory and patience, made independent. Still, slow, absolute. Worshipped by scholars and historians. Believes memory preserved is the truest form of prayer.
Worshipped by: The Stillkeep. Temples: stone libraries.
Solvara
Goddess of Secrets, Shadow & Hidden Truth
Born from the Partition — Nara's shadow-self, made independent. Neither dark nor light. Holds something back from the other gods for reasons only she understands. Her followers believe she is keeping the most dangerous secret in Anavale.
Worshipped by: The Veilborn. Their practices are not public knowledge.
Grak
The Fallen — God of Order, now of Silence
Once the god of structure and law. Fell during the Partition into obsession with absolute control, then into grief, then into something there is no clean word for. Not publicly worshipped. Those who serve Grak's will call it "order" and "efficiency." They are not incorrect that it produces a kind of order.
Symbol: a cracked grey circle — once perfect.
Region

Caparia

The central heartlands of Pogglewog

Caparia is the heart of Pogglewog — the largest, most diverse, and most politically complex region on the continent. It is home to lush meadows, sparkling inland seas, and the most colorful cities in Anavale. The sun is overhead here, which the Brightcreed considers cosmically appropriate.

"In Caparia, color maintenance is not pride. It is law. The fine for a faded storefront is modest. The social consequences are not." — A Bunari merchant, describing Solenveil
Notable Places
Solenveil
Capital · Caldenric Accord
The most colorful city in Anavale. Color maintenance is legally required. Faded buildings receive restoration crews within a week. Home of the Gigglegloom Conclave's primary offices and the famous Mirrenflow promenade.
Goober Bounce Meadows
Geographic Feature
Vast central meadowlands famous for their extraordinary density of Bounce Beetles. The meadows produce a near-constant "boing" sound on warm afternoons. Visitors find this either delightful or deeply disorienting. Usually both.
Sillywiggles Meadows
Geographic Feature
Rolling southern meadows known for Gigglegloom wildflower surges in spring. During peak season, wildflowers here bloom in colors that technically shouldn't exist. Botanical scholars visit annually. They leave confused and happy.
Prismhold
City · Chroma Bureau
The Chroma Bureau's city-state at the exact geographic center of Caparia. Every Gigglegloom license in Anavale passes through here. Also home to the Prior Stone — a monument of pure white raw Gigglegloom that the Bureau has surrounded with 44 forms' worth of access restrictions.
Doopu Peaks
Mountain Range
The eastern mountain range, home to the Brightwall — a cliff face where all four Gigglegloom types surge simultaneously at every equinox. The most spectacular natural Gigglegloom event in Anavale. Visible from twenty miles away.
Reveltown
Town · Northwest Caparia
Home base of the traveling festival guild known as The Revel. Perpetually mid-festival. Nobody who lives there knows what day it is. Visitors are welcomed immediately and enthusiastically into whatever is currently happening.

👑 Nations & Governments ▼

Caparia is unified by The Pogglewog Confederation, a 300-year-old alliance of six nations that share trade routes, enforce peace, and celebrate Brightcreed festivals together:

  • The Solenmere: The central representative democracy (Caldenric Accord) known for highly colorful cities and trained "Color Reader" mediators.
  • The Bunari: An eastern coastal merchant council where a significant portion of the population lives freely on ships.
  • The Zippan: A western hill nation run by a Council of Guilds, boasting the absolute best food and undisputed festival champions.
  • The Dingurei: An eastern forest nation governed by an Assembly of Scribes, organizing their cities around massive libraries.
  • The Janiveth: A southern mountain nation run by an elder clan assembly that prizes endurance and maintains a 400-year "Winter Count" color record.
  • The Opuri: A northwestern forest nation guided by a Council of Listeners who live alongside an ancient forest Mosskin.
🏛️ Prominent Organizations ▼
  • The Caldenric Accord: The beloved, fair, and slightly indecisive representative democracy governing the central Caparian nations.
  • The Chroma Bureau (Gigglegloom Conclave): The strict arcane theocracy in Prismhold that governs every single magic license in Anavale.
  • The Formery: The oldest, most delightfully bureaucratic organization in the world — they have an office in Confederation Hall that nobody invited them to set up.
  • The Revel: A traveling festival guild that operates out of Reveltown.
🐾 Flora & Fauna ▼
  • Cute Companions: The region is filled with tiny, round pastel squirrels called Fluffets, humming green Bumble Frogs, ricocheting Bounce Beetles, and Pocketmoles — pets that locate sad people and sit on their feet to comfort them.
  • Impossible Flowers: In the Sillywiggles Meadows, spring Gigglegloom surges cause wildflowers to bloom in vibrant, glowing colors that technically shouldn't exist.
🍭 Food & Drink ▼
  • The Zippan nation creates the best food in Anavale.
  • Festival favorites include Zippan Fizz-Tarts (pastries with popping jam), floating mugs of Cloudwhump Cotton-Cider, and voice-changing Gigglegloom Gummies.
🗺️ Points of Interest ▼
  • Solenveil: The sprawling capital city where maintaining the color of buildings is legally required.
  • Prismhold & The Prior Stone: The geographic center of Caparia, housing a restricted monument of pure white, raw Gigglegloom magic.
  • The Brightwall: A massive cliff in the Doopu Peaks where all four types of magic surge simultaneously at the equinox.
  • Reveltown: A town perpetually caught mid-festival, where nobody knows what day it is.
  • The Partition Scar: A crack in the bedrock in Dingu Forest where raw magic seeps into the world.
🌑 The Main Threat ▼
  • Drak: The Vareth's twisted sorcerer lieutenant who operates within Caparia. He obsessively corrupts the Gigglegloom and hides his research in a secret archive.
  • The Dark Zones: Areas like Greyveil Hollow and The Grasping Ledge are Drakhold outposts where the color has been completely drained, leaving a flat, joyless grey.

The Solenmere

The bureaucratic and highly colorful heart of Caparia

The Solenmere is the political and cultural heart of Caparia. Situated along the great Mirrenflow river, it is the most sprawling and densely populated nation in the region, and its capital, Solenveil, is considered the most colorful city in all of Anavale. Color maintenance is not merely custom here — it is law. A faded building receives a restoration crew within a single week. The social consequences for allowing a storefront to fade are considerably harsher than the modest fine.

Government

The Solenmere is governed by the Caldenric Accord, a beloved 24-member representative democracy with a rotating Chancellor. They are widely considered kind and fair — and occasionally hilariously indecisive. Population numbers are meticulously (if inefficiently) tracked by The Formery, who have maintained an uninvited office inside Confederation Hall for sixty years.

Culture

The Solenmere is famous for the Welcome Table custom, in which colored cloth is displayed to signal social availability to guests. Disputes are settled not by courts but by trained Color Readers — skilled mediators who read emotional states through color and guide conflicting parties toward resolution. They hold a politely competitive rivalry with the Aurentum monarchy of Sohot, and view slower governments such as Nombi's Deepchill Accord as slightly exhausting.

Points of Interest

The Mirrenflow promenade is the grand social artery of Solenveil. Confederation Hall — the seat of the Pogglewog Confederation — is here, and hosts representatives of all six Confederation nations. Note: Prismhold is geographically within Solenmere's territory, but operates as a fully independent city-state under the Chroma Bureau.

Threats & Shadows

The Nimblewood thieves' guild operates quietly within Solenmere, adhering strictly to their code of never stealing from those who cannot afford it. Criminal activity is otherwise mostly non-violent — colorful vandalism, bureaucratic forgery, and magical licensing fraud.

The Bunari

The free-spirited maritime nation of the eastern coast

The Bunari are a seafaring people who have built an entire national identity around the ocean, personal freedom, and perpetual movement. Their territory stretches along the eastern Rabbit Coast and Bunbun Bay, but a highly significant portion of their population does not live on land at all — they live permanently on ships, in flotilla-towns that drift and regroup with the seasons.

Government

The nation is governed by a Merchant Council with an executive Harbor Master. Their population is famously difficult to track accurately, as census-takers must first find the ships. The Bunari view the rest of the Confederation as slightly too grounded, but happily manage the bulk of Caparia's sea trade.

Culture

If a Bunari citizen stays in one physical location for more than two years, the community stages concerned check-ins. Freedom of movement is not just valued — it is considered a measure of personal health. The Bunari are friendly and open, holding a subtle bias against overly strict land-based laws and viewing those who never travel as slightly tragic.

Points of Interest

Mirrenport is Caparia's major port city. The Shimmer Shoals — a brilliantly glowing, bioluminescent section of Bunbun Bay — are considered one of the most beautiful natural sights in the region. The bay is home to the semi-sapient Shimmer Rays, who glow in patterns that some Brightcreed scholars believe carry meaning.

Threats & Shadows

The Grusk underworld occasionally uses Bunari ports to quietly move Drains and extracted color across borders. Sailors periodically report the unsettling Stillborn Tide — grey bioluminescence creeping into unmonitored shipping lanes, a quiet sign that the Vareth's reach is testing the edges of the coast.

The Zippan

The proud, joyful culinary masters of the western hills

If you have eaten well on your travels through Caparia, you have almost certainly eaten Zippan food. The western hill nation is not shy about this. They will tell you. At length. At every meal. The Zippan are farmers, brewers, builders, and craftspeople who have elevated the preparation of food and the hosting of festivals into their highest cultural and spiritual practice.

Government

The Zippan are governed by a Council of Guilds, structured so that no single guild holds more than three seats. This produces lively, argumentative governance that tends to resolve itself when someone brings food. Their major hub is Bumbleton, surrounded by idyllic farming villages.

Culture

Craft pride is enormous and completely non-negotiable. The Zippan resolve all disagreements through The Craft Challenge — both parties make something, the community judges, and the matter is closed. They view sloppiness as a character flaw and anyone who insults a crafted good risks a lengthy, passionate response. They are undisputed Brightcreed festival champions across the Confederation and will accept no debate on this point.

Points of Interest

The Goober Bounce Meadows — vast central meadowlands famous for an extraordinary density of Bounce Beetles — produce a near-constant "boing" sound on warm afternoons. Festival specialties include Zippan Fizz-Tarts (pastries with popping jam), floating mugs of Cloudwhump Cotton-Cider, and voice-changing Gigglegloom Gummies.

Threats & Shadows

The Breth Chaine — a creature slavery ring — attempts to operate quietly on the rural fringes of Zippan territory, poaching healthy creatures. Dimming is kept effectively at bay by the sheer volume of festivals, which actively push the grey back. A Zippan celebration is, whether intentionally or not, an act of magical resistance.

The Dingurei

The scholarly, question-driven archivists of the eastern forests

The Dingurei have lived in the Dingu Forest for three centuries, studying it. They have also been studying the Partition Scar — a literal crack in the bedrock of the forest floor where raw Gigglegloom seeps directly into the world — for just as long. They have not finished. They are not sure they ever will. They consider this acceptable.

Government

Governance is handled by the Assembly of Scribes, where representation is earned strictly through scholarly achievement. Children are not given standard names — instead, they are each given a question they are expected to spend their entire life answering.

Culture

The Dingurei maintain more complete Gigglegloom surge records than the Chroma Bureau in Prismhold, a fact the Bureau acknowledges with diplomatic discomfort. An ongoing academic cold war simmers between the two institutions over who holds the most accurate magical records. The Dingurei are highly respected across the Confederation, though viewed as slightly detached.

Points of Interest

Vellum is the capital city, built entirely around the Great Index — the most comprehensive magical library in Anavale, organized by a classification system so intricate that three full-time Scribes are devoted solely to maintaining its table of contents. Inkwell is the major supporting town, dedicated entirely to producing the paper and binding materials that Vellum consumes at an alarming rate. The Partition Scar — a crack in the bedrock where raw Gigglegloom seeps directly into the world — has been under continuous Dingurei study for three centuries. Scholars from across Anavale come to observe. The Dingurei issue access permits. The waiting list is eleven years long.

Threats & Shadows

Drak, the Vareth's twisted sorcerer lieutenant, operates somewhere deep within Dingurei borders — maintaining a secret, hidden archive of corrupted Gigglegloom research. Localized pockets of Stage 1 Fading have appeared near his concealed outposts. The Dingurei have noticed the anomalies. They are studying them carefully. They have not yet realized what is causing them.

The Janiveth

The resilient, mountain-dwelling record keepers of the south

The Janiveth are a people defined by endurance. They live in high-altitude fortresses and deep-valley clan settlements in the Jani Forest and the mountain range that marks Caparia's southern border with Sohot. They do not celebrate easily. They do not rush decisions. They have survived longer than any other mountain civilization on the continent, and they consider this sufficient evidence that their approach is correct.

Government

The nation is governed by an Elder Clan Assembly requiring a two-thirds majority to pass decisions — more flexible than Nombi's full consensus, but not by much. They control the mountain passes between Caparia and the desert south, a geopolitical leverage they exercise with great, deliberate restraint.

Culture

The Janiveth maintain the Winter Count — a 400-year unbroken record in which every single year is recorded as a single, community-chosen color. This living color archive is both historical record and magical instrument: it lets the Janiveth notice even the subtlest drop in regional color saturation across generations. They view the central Caparian nations — Solenmere, Zippan — as slightly frivolous and soft. They say this with the quiet certainty of people who have never needed rescuing.

Points of Interest

The treacherous mountain passes leading into the Desolate Wastes of Sohot are the Janiveth's most strategically significant feature. These passes are the primary overland route between the two regions. The Janiveth have kept them open, protected, and carefully monitored for centuries.

Threats & Shadows

Unaligned smugglers and occasional Grusk operatives attempt to move illicit color or Voidblush through the southern passes, bypassing the Janiveth checkpoints. They are rarely successful. The Janiveth are very patient, and the mountains are very tall.

The Opuri

The quiet, balanced stewards of the northwestern woods

The Opuri have lived in the Opu Forest as long as the forest has existed. They are the smallest nation in the Confederation and the quietest. They do not seek attention. They do not expand their borders. They listen — to the wind, to the leaves, to the slow, patient communications of The Patient One, a 10,000-year-old Mosskin who has accumulated so much Gigglegloom over its existence that it has developed personality, intention, and something approaching wisdom.

Government

The Opuri are guided by the Council of Listeners — twelve members chosen not by election or appointment, but by the apparent preference of the Patient One, communicated through leaf arrangements. The Chroma Bureau has asked, several times, for clarification on how this process works. The Opuri have not provided it.

Culture

The Opuri do not worship the Mosskin. They do not study it or attempt to interview it. They live alongside it as a neighbor — respectfully, quietly, without demanding anything in return. This is the closest human-Mosskin relationship known to exist anywhere in Anavale. Their culture is defined by deep listening and ecological harmony, and they carry a strong distrust of anyone who treats the Gigglegloom purely as a mechanical resource to be extracted.

Points of Interest

Mosswhisper Grove is the known location of the Patient One — the oldest and most aware Mosskin in Caparia. It communicates through leaf arrangements, which the Council of Listeners has spent generations learning to interpret. The Opuri are the only nation in Anavale with a statistically equal distribution of all four Gigglegloom types. The Chroma Bureau considers this impossible. The Opuri have not commented.

Threats & Shadows

The Vareth and the Grusk actively avoid Opuri territory. The presence of the 10,000-year-old Mosskin acts as a natural deterrent — the Dimming cannot take hold in a place so thoroughly saturated with living Gigglegloom. External poachers are the only real crime issue. They are handled swiftly and without drama. The forest does not forget.

Region

Nombi

The frozen north, where the aurora speaks

Nombi is cold, densely forested, and old. The kind of old that doesn't announce itself but sits quietly in the roots of trees and the cracks of rocks, watching. The aurora lights here — shifting greens and blues and golds — are considered by most Nombi cultures to be a form of divine communication. They disagree about what, specifically, is being communicated, which has produced three distinct religious and philosophical traditions and a great deal of excellent debate.

"The Nombi don't say much. But they are always listening. I have found this more comforting than I expected." — A Caparian traveller's journal
Notable Places
Coldmere
Capital · Deepchill Accord
Seat of Nombi's governing clan assembly. Has been deciding its national flag for 60 years. Extremely warm indoors. The best stew in Anavale. Visitors are strongly encouraged to arrive before the first snow.
The Endless Forest
Geographic Feature
A vast central forest that appears to have no edge, despite cartographers' insistence that it must. Creatures here are particularly vivid. The tree density produces a particular quality of light that visitors describe as "like being inside color itself."
Sky Islands
Geographic Feature · Above the Glacial Sea
Floating islands above the Glacial Sea, accessible only via aurora wind currents that require specific navigational knowledge. Home to Cloudwhumps and various sky-going creatures. The view from the islands is, by all accounts, unreasonable.
The Pale Ring
Gigglegloom Surge Site
A Steelfist surge site in Nombi's interior where cold violet rings appear in the ice each winter. Clan elders read them as messages from Thyun. The Stillkeep sends observers annually. They disagree on the interpretation.
Region

Sohot

The blazing south, where ancient things persist

Sohot is a land of extremes — blazing desert, ancient ruins, underground cities of extraordinary complexity, and a monarchy that throws the most lavish parties on the continent while quietly managing a crisis it hasn't named yet. It is also the site of some of Anavale's oldest civilization, and the ruins of that civilization are everywhere, as a constant reminder that even very grand things end.

"The Auvari built everything and then stopped. We live in what they left. This is either humbling or clarifying, depending on how the morning has gone." — Auvari Remnance elder, Aurentum City
Notable Places
Aurentum City
Capital · The Aurentum
Seat of Sohot's monarchy. Lavish, colorful, perfumed, and loud. The royal palace has more pets than staff. The court fashion is extraordinary. Something may or may not be wrong with the Queen's gowns. Nobody is discussing this officially.
Sunharbor
Major Port · Bumti Bay
Sohot's largest port. Spice and color trade flows through here. Gerald the Bumti Jelly returns to the harbor every summer without fail. The harbor master has known Gerald for forty years and considers him reliable company.
The Sizzling Dunes
Geographic Feature
The central desert. Extraordinarily hot, home to Sandgliders and Sunslugs, and traversed by the Vokrath people who have been navigating it for generations using methods they find it funny that outsiders cannot replicate.
The Bubblespring
Sacred Site · Brightcreed
A Bubbleseed surge site near Bumti Bay where a hot spring produces faintly gold-tinted water. The second-holiest Brightcreed site in Anavale. Pilgrimage season is crowded but festive.
Traveller's Note
A location in the Ashfall Wastes called the Quiet Pillar is noted in several travel accounts as a place where color ceases to exist within 100 feet. All compendium editors recommend against visiting it for recreational purposes.
Region

Jugabi

The jungle southwest, alive beyond counting

Jugabi is the Dodooti Rainforest, and the Dodooti Rainforest is arguably the most alive place in Anavale. Its canopy, when seen from above, looks like a second ocean. Below that canopy, everything is in conversation with everything else — every creature, every tree, every Gigglegloom surge is part of a system so complex that the scholars who study it agree primarily on this: they do not understand it yet.

Two nations share this forest. They have been arguing about the right way to live in it for eighty years. Both are correct about some things. Neither would admit this.

"The forest has an opinion about you. Give it time and you'll figure out what it is." — Verdathi elder, deep Dodooti
Notable Places
Dodooti Rainforest
Geographic Feature
Ancient, vast, and alive in ways that exceed easy description. The canopy produces colors not easily named. Rootsingers — ancient creatures that resemble trees until they move — are believed to live here. The Greenvast Tribes will not cut the oldest trees.
Bloom Hollow
Sacred Site · Gigglegloom Surge
A Bubbleseed surge site deep in the Dodooti where flowers bloom in colors without names. The second-holiest Brightcreed site in Anavale, drawing pilgrims annually. The flowers do not survive being picked. This is considered a message.
Tanglevine
Town · Northern Coast
Where Jugabi meets Caparia's trade network. Chaotic, colorful, and extraordinarily fragrant from the combination of jungle flowers and market spices. The best place on Pogglewog to hear three languages spoken simultaneously.
Mumblewump
Village · Deep Jungle
A village that exists entirely in the jungle canopy, accessible only by rope bridge. People who were not born there rarely find it twice. People who were born there find this fact quietly hilarious.
Bestiary

Common Creatures

Those you are likely to meet on any given afternoon

Anavale's creatures are everywhere, and they are paying attention to you. This is not threatening. It is, in fact, rather wonderful once you get used to it. The following entries cover creatures a traveller on Pogglewog will encounter regularly. They are described here as observed, not as classified — the Chroma Bureau's creature classification system involves forty-seven forms and this compendium does not have the patience for that.

Fluffets
Tiny round squirrels — pastel fur, oversized ears
Make a sound described universally as "bwip." Startled by almost everything — loud noises, sudden movements, unexpected kindness, their own reflections. Will bring acorn gifts to people they decide they like. The decision process is opaque but the outcome is unmistakable.
Practical note: Fluffet acorn gifts should be accepted graciously. Declining them causes visible distress.
Bumble Frogs
Round green frogs — hum instead of croaking
Hum at a frequency that harmonizes naturally with other Bumble Frogs, producing spontaneous choirs in any area with a sufficient population. Will attempt to join any song being sung nearby. Their pitch accuracy is surprisingly good. Their timing is their own business.
Warning indicator: if the Bumble Frogs in an area have gone quiet, something is wrong.
Tinywings
Thumb-sized dragons — enormous wings, breathe tiny sparks
Cannot fly straight. This is a fact. They are aware of this fact. They disagree with it. If you observe a Tinywing flying in a curve and mention this, expect a heated discussion that will eventually conclude with the Tinywing flying away in a curve while projecting an air of dignity.
Do not bet against a Tinywing. They always arrive eventually.
Bounce Beetles
Shiny colorful beetles — ricochet off surfaces
Travel in packs. Navigate by ricocheting off walls, trees, people, and each other, producing a constant soft "boing" sound. Their routes are completely chaotic. Their destinations are somehow always correct. Scholars have been studying this for decades and remain unable to explain it.
Bounce Beetles in an area indicate good Gigglegloom health. Many Bounce Beetles indicate excellent health and also mild chaos.
Pocketmoles
Small velvet-furred moles — common household companion
The most common pet in Caparia. Cannot see well but have extraordinary emotional sensitivity — will locate any sad person in a room and sit on their foot until acknowledged. Collect small colored objects and leave them in their owner's shoes as gifts. Nobody asked for this. Nobody has ever successfully stopped it.
Having a Pocketmole sit on your foot is generally considered a kindness, even if it doesn't feel like one.
Sparklings
Tiny hummingbird-like creatures — move as streaks of colored light
So fast they are essentially invisible in motion, leaving only color trails — gold for Bubbleseed, teal for Featherflow, violet for Steelfist, red-orange for Flamerage. Where Sparklings cluster, the Gigglegloom is healthy. Where they avoid, it is not. The Wanderkeep uses their distribution as a primary health indicator.
A Sparkling that stops moving and hovers directly in front of you is an unusual event. Pay attention to where it goes next.

Rare & Mysterious

Those you may meet once, or never, or in dreams

Anavale contains things that resist easy categorization. The following entries are compiled from eyewitness accounts, Stillkeep records, Brightcreed theological texts, and one extremely detailed letter written to the Chroma Bureau that the Bureau declined to respond to.

Hollowmoths
Large ash-grey moths — mirror eyes, native to Dimmed places
Unlike other grey creatures, Hollowmoths did not fade. They were born grey, in places that had been Dimmed long enough for something new to grow from the absence. They feed on the residual warmth of color that recently left a location. Children are inexplicably unafraid of them. Hollowmoths will land on a child's outstretched hand and sit quietly, wings slowly opening and closing, as if remembering something.
If you see a Hollowmoth somewhere it wasn't before: the Dimming has already arrived. The moth is the announcement.
Mirrorwolves
Wolves — fur reflects the observer's strongest emotion
A person in grief sees a grey, hollow animal. A person in joy sees something vivid and luminous. A person who is afraid sees something they will not describe afterward. They are the same creature in all cases — ordinary wolves with extraordinary fur. Travel in packs of exactly seven. Have never been recorded attacking a creature that is genuinely happy.
The Brightcreed considers this significant. The Mirrorwolves are indifferent to the Brightcreed's opinion.
The Gilded Tortoise
Ancient tortoise — trees grow on its shell
An ancient tortoise so large that small trees grow on its shell. Walks a route taking approximately forty years to complete, crossing both Sohot and southern Caparia. Finding it is considered the greatest possible omen of good fortune in Brightcreed tradition. Several people have built small seasonal homes on its shell. The Tortoise does not appear to mind.
At least one Mosskin accumulation has apparently decided the Tortoise is a valid location. The Tortoise has not commented.
Loomhares
Rabbits — glowing geometric markings, sometimes phase through walls
Overloaded with Gigglegloom — not corrupted, simply overwhelmed. Their markings shift color with their moods. They sometimes pass through solid objects, which they appear not to notice. Behavior is completely unpredictable. May explode with confetti. Have been known to briefly speak. What they say rarely makes sense in the moment and sometimes makes sense later.
The Chroma Bureau advises not making eye contact. The compendium editors suggest that is going to be difficult.
The Unseen
No confirmed description — ancient beyond memory
No confirmed description exists. Witnesses report different things: a warmth, a shape, a feeling of great age. Before an Unseen passes through an area, animals go very still — not frightened, attentive. After, one object in the area is left changed: not damaged, not moved, but somehow different in a way that defies explanation. A door with new grain. A stone geometrically smooth on one face. A child's drawing with one additional figure, always facing away.
The Chroma Bureau has filed Form 1-A seventeen times. Each time it is returned stamped: ENTITY DECLINES TO RESPOND.
Society

Notable Organizations

Guilds, institutions, and groups worth knowing about

The Gigglegloom Conclave
Magic licensing authority — answers to no government
Governs all Gigglegloom use in Anavale. Issues licenses, warnings, and penalties. Answers to no political authority. Enforces Gigglegloom law on anyone, regardless of rank. Its secret military force, the Chromeguard, wears armor that shifts color in patterns unique to each member — impossible to impersonate. The Conclave's city-state, Prismhold, sits at the exact center of Caparia.
The Wanderkeep
Wandering protectors of the Gigglegloom
Wandering guardians who travel Anavale patching Gigglegloom corruption, monitoring Dimming progression, and generally doing what needs doing in places that have been overlooked. They have no fixed home. They are very tired. They are very committed. They track Sparkling distribution and Bumble Frog behavior as primary health indicators.
The Kindpact
Ethical creature traders
Ethical traders of creatures, with strict codes of care and transport. Their practitioners can assess a creature's Gigglegloom health by coat, scale, or feather color alone. Maintain a standing bounty on every Breth Chaine operation. Share their list of known color-extraction practitioners freely with anyone who asks.
The Revel
Traveling festival guild
Traveling circuses, performers, bards, and acrobats. Everywhere there is joy, The Revel is nearby. Every performance in a Fading town leaves the buildings slightly more saturated for days afterward. Some members know this is not a coincidence. All of them feel it. Rumored to have other purposes. Officially denied.
The Prism Exchange
Traders of magical pigments and color
Traders of rare pigments, dyes, and hues that hold Gigglegloom within them. Their rarest product — Voidblush — can temporarily reverse early-stage Dimming with a single drop. They are very protective of their stock. They have survived seven assassination attempts. They remain open for business.
The Formery
Bureau of Thorough Consideration — the oldest organization in Anavale
FORM 1-A: RECEIVED... EVENTUALLY
The oldest organization in Anavale. Not the oldest active organization — the oldest organization, full stop. Its founding predates the Partition, the Auvari empire, every government and religion currently operating in Anavale. Nobody knows what it was originally designed to do. It processes requests, charges modest fees, and issues forms for situations that may never arise. It has been doing this since before memory, which is why everyone tolerates it with the patience one extends to something that has clearly been here longer than everything else and will be here after everything else has gone.
The clerks are always polite. They have seen everything before. They will see everything again.
The Nimblewood
International thieves' guild — has a code
International thieves' guild with a surprisingly firm code of honor: never steal from those who cannot afford to lose it, never work for the grey. Principled and professional. The current guildmaster is believed to be a very clever squirrel. This has not been confirmed. It has not been denied.
Hearsay

Rumours & Hearsay

Unverified. Potentially significant. Definitely interesting.

The following accounts have been gathered from various sources across Pogglewog. The editorial committee takes no responsibility for their accuracy. The editorial committee does note, however, that several rumors from the previous edition that were considered implausible have since been confirmed. The committee is watching these with interest.

Source: Aurentum City — merchant quarter, overheard
"The Queen's gowns have been looking different lately. The court says it's the dye lots. Three seamstresses have been dismissed this month. I've been buying from that dyer for twelve years. The dye is fine."
Source: Eastern Caparia road — traveller's account
"Someone left a black prism carved into the doorframe of the old mill on the east road, just past Bumbleton. Nobody saw who did it. The miller moved out the next morning. Didn't take anything with him. The mill has been empty for three weeks. Nobody has gone in."
Source: Jani Forest, Caparia — Brightcreed ranger report
"The Bumble Frogs in the southern Jani Forest have gone quiet. All of them. Over a stretch of about two miles. The frogs north of the line are fine. The frogs south of the line are silent. The line didn't exist a month ago."
Source: Prismhold — Formery clerk, unofficial communication
"We received a form this week in a script none of our senior staff could read. We sent it to the Stillkeep. They sent it back with a note saying the script predates the current calendar system by a significant margin and they would need more time. The interesting part: the form was a renewal. Someone is renewing something."
Source: Reveltown, northwest Caparia — festival attendee
"A Hollowmoth appeared in the middle of the Dawnburst celebration last week. In the festival district. In full afternoon light. It landed on a child's shoulder and stayed there for almost an hour. The child said it felt sad. Not scared — sad. She stayed very still until it left. I've been thinking about it since."
Source: Sunharbor docks, Sohot — dockworker, unnamed
"Three Kindpact inspectors came through the lower docks last week and they weren't inspecting the usual things. They were asking about water color. Whether we'd noticed Ashcreek looking different. Whether any of the Shimmer Rays in Bunbun Bay had stopped glowing. I told them no. They wrote it down and looked like that wasn't the answer they were hoping for."
Source: Nombi — Tumblesnow town notice board
"The Aurora Moths didn't fly last night. The aurora was visible. Bright, actually. The moths were present — they were on the branches, wings closed, watching. They just didn't fly. Old Morra says she's never seen that in seventy years of moth-watching. She doesn't say what it means. She's been very quiet since."
