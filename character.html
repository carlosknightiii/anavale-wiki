Stage 4 of 5 · Alignment

Your Compass

Not a cage. Think of it as the compass your character tends to follow when nobody's watching. It can shift over time.

Brightward
Lawful Good in the Player's Handbook
You believe the world is worth protecting and that the best way to protect it is to be someone others can count on. Rules matter because they keep people safe.
"I keep my word even when it costs me something. Especially then."
"I believe most people are trying their best. I've been wrong about this. I still believe it."
"When something bad happens, my first question is: what can I do?"
"I find it genuinely hard to understand people who don't care whether they're good."
Colorful
Chaotic Good in the Player's Handbook
You want to do right by people but you've never been very good at following someone else's idea of how. Rules feel like cages even when they're sensible ones.
"I make decisions fast and feel bad about the wrong ones later. This is a feature, not a bug."
"I don't think the rules were written for situations like this one."
"I care deeply about people and very little about systems."
"I've been told I'm a lot. I've decided to take that as a compliment."
Greywarden
True Neutral in the Player's Handbook
You see all sides. You weigh things carefully. You don't think the world is simple enough to be neatly divided into light and dark.
"I try to understand a situation fully before deciding what I think about it."
"I don't owe anyone my loyalty just because they asked for it."
"I've seen good people do terrible things for good reasons. I try to remember that."
"I'm not disengaged. I'm careful. There's a difference."
Steelbound
Lawful Neutral in the Player's Handbook
Order is not a cage. Order is how things work. You respect hierarchies, honor agreements, and follow through on commitments.
"I do what I said I would do. This is not complicated."
"I respect authority when it earns respect. I follow it even when it hasn't, because the alternative is worse."
"I believe in fairness more than I believe in kindness."
"I've been told I'm inflexible. I prefer: reliable."
Ashwalker
Chaotic Neutral in the Player's Handbook
You do what works for you, and you try to be honest about that. You're not cruel — but you don't pretend to have obligations you don't feel.
"I make decisions based on what's actually in front of me."
"I'm loyal to people I've chosen to be loyal to. That list is short and carefully maintained."
"I don't think I owe the world anything it hasn't given me first."
"I've done things I'm not proud of. I'd probably do them again."
